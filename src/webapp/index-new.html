How to Use This Tool

What This Tool Does

We analyze privacy policies and terms of service to identify potentially concerning clauses in plain language. You can choose:

  • Quick Scan (2 min): Highlights critical issues only
  • Full Analysis (6 min): Comprehensive review of all findings

Understanding Confidence Scores

Each finding includes a confidence score indicating how certain we are about the finding:

  • 95%+ (Green): Highly confident in this finding
  • 70-95% (Yellow): Probable finding, may need your review
  • <70% (Orange): Uncertain—we recommend reviewing the source text

Limitations

This tool cannot identify every issue, and laws change frequently. For critical decisions, consult with a qualified attorney.
