Let's understand what you're signing up for

Before you agree to a company's privacy policy or terms of service, you deserve to know what you're agreeing to—without reading pages of legal language.

We'll read it for you and explain anything that might affect you in plain English.

Here's what will happen

  1. You'll paste the legal text (or give us the website link where it lives)
  2. We'll read through it using trusted privacy frameworks and legal standards
  3. We'll show you anything important—things like: Can they sell your data? Do you lose rights if there's a problem? Are there surprise fees?

This usually takes 2–6 minutes depending on how thorough you want to be.
