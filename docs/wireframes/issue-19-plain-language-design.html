What was checked: The words in this policy. How it describes data collection, sharing, tracking, AI/automated decisions, and rights under California (CCPA/CPRA) and EU (GDPR) rules.

What wasn't checked:
  • What permissions the Facebook app actually requests on a phone (camera, microphone, contacts, location). Those live in Settings → Apps → Facebook → Permissions.
  • Whether Facebook's real-world practices match what this policy says. Only the document itself was analyzed.
Data what's collected
  1. 1
    The policy indicates photos, posts, and messages are collected, including from third-party apps and websites connected via Meta business tools.
  2. 2
    For a child on the platform, biometric-adjacent signals (face-shape data used in photo tagging) may be collected under separate settings.
Data use how it's used
  1. 1
    Photos, posts, and activity may be used to train Facebook's AI systems. A full opt-out is not offered while the account remains active.
  2. 2
    Data flows automatically between Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp for advertising and personalization, regardless of whether a child has signed up for all three.
Terms of use the agreement itself
  1. 1
    By using the service, agreement to this policy is treated as given. No explicit opt-in step is described.
  2. 2
    Account deletion is available, though the policy notes some data may be retained for "safety and security" reasons with no defined time limit.
Privacy rights what can still be exercised
  1. 1
    California residents can opt out of data sale/share. An equivalent right is not described for other regions.
  2. 2
    Data download and deletion requests can be filed through account Settings. Response timelines are not specified.

Some things worth considering for a child
  • Facebook's Family Center offers supervision tools for parents managing a teen's account.
  • For accounts of children under 13, a report can be filed via Facebook's underage account form and separately with the FTC under COPPA.
  • The Teen Privacy Checkup inside Facebook settings walks through recommendations for stricter defaults on ad targeting and off-Facebook activity.
  • Turning off cross-app data sharing between Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp is available in the Meta Accounts Center under Personal Details.
