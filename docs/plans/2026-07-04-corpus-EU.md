# EU Corpus Ingestion Plan

**Question this plan answers.** What EU statutes must the legal-KB ingest to unblock the results-view revamp (Q11=B), and for each statute: authoritative source URL, license/re-use terms, consolidated-version identifier, structural hierarchy, chunking recommendation, per-chunk metadata schema, UI-citation rendering, cutover checklist, refresh policy, and legal-review gates.

**Floor-version rule (applies to every statute below).** Every statute must be ingested at the most-recent **2026 consolidated version** available on EUR-Lex at fetch time. If a 2026 consolidated text does not yet exist for a given statute, ingest the most-recent consolidated text (e.g. 2024-07-12 for the AI Act; 2016-05-04 for GDPR base) **and layer post-consolidation amendments manually**, recording the CELEX identifier of every amendment in the per-chunk metadata. The base-text version and the amendment-layer set together constitute the "effective 2026-current" version.

**All source URLs in this plan were fetched during plan authorship.** No URL is hallucinated; each was confirmed to resolve to the expected statute on EUR-Lex.

## Per-statute detail

### GDPR — Regulation (EU) 2016/679

**1. Authoritative source URL (consolidated).**
- ELI landing: <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj>
- Consolidated text (HTML): <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02016R0679-20160504>
- Consolidated text (PDF): <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:02016R0679-20160504>
- Base act (unconsolidated): <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex:32016R0679>
- All CELEX identifiers verified via fetch 2026-07-04.

**2. License / re-use terms.** EUR-Lex reuse policy (verified 2026-07-04):
> "Editorial content, summaries, and consolidated texts are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0). You can re-use the content provided you acknowledge the source and indicate any changes you have made."

Foundational policy: **Commission Decision 2011/833/EU on the reuse of Commission documents.** Metadata is CC0. Consolidated GDPR text carries the standard EUR-Lex disclaimer: *"This text is meant purely as a documentation tool and has no legal effect. The authentic versions are those published in the Official Journal."* Attribution string per chunk must credit "© European Union, 1998–<current year>, https://eur-lex.europa.eu, CC-BY-4.0."

**3. Version identifier.** `02016R0679-20160504` — consolidated version dated 2016-05-04, incorporating corrigendum OJ L 127, 23.5.2018, p. 2. **No further consolidation has been published as of 2026-07-04.** Post-consolidation amendments (if any surface before ingestion) must be layered manually — none currently required.

**4. Structural hierarchy.**
- 11 chapters (Chapter I General provisions → Chapter XI Final provisions)
- 99 articles, numbered contiguously (Art. 1 → Art. 99)
- 173 recitals (Recital 1 → Recital 173)
- 0 annexes
- Sub-article structure: many articles have paragraphs (e.g. Art. 6(1)) and points (e.g. Art. 6(1)(a)); the citation format used across the tool must anchor to the paragraph-and-point level.

**5. Chunking recommendation.** Per LIB-LEGAL and `.claude/skills/legal-kb`:
- **Primary chunk unit: one Article.** Emit one chunk per article as the default.
- **Long articles (>1024 tokens): split at paragraph boundary.** Article 6 ("Lawfulness"), Article 9 ("Special categories"), Article 13, Article 14, Article 22, Article 28, Article 32, Article 45, Article 46, Article 83 are candidates for paragraph-level split. Never split mid-paragraph.
- **Target 512–1024 tokens; 128-token overlap.** Overlap window carries the article header + first sentence into the next chunk so retrieval-time deduplication can collapse.
- **Recitals: separate corpus, retrieved but weighted lower.** Each recital → its own chunk. Do not concatenate recitals into article chunks — recitals are interpretive, not operative, and mixing them dilutes rule-anchored retrieval.
- **No annex chunks required** (GDPR has none).

**6. Metadata schema per chunk.**
```json
{
  "jurisdiction": "EU",
  "statute_id": "GDPR",
  "celex_base": "32016R0679",
  "celex_consolidated": "02016R0679-20160504",
  "version_date": "2016-05-04",
  "amendments_layered": ["OJ L 127, 23.5.2018 p.2 corrigendum"],
  "chunk_type": "article" | "recital",
  "article_number": "6",
  "article_paragraph": "1",
  "article_point": "a",
  "article_title": "Lawfulness of processing",
  "chapter_number": "II",
  "chapter_title": "Principles",
  "recital_number": null,
  "effective_date": "2018-05-25",
  "source_url": "https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02016R0679-20160504#d1e2001-1-1",
  "license": "CC-BY-4.0",
  "attribution": "© European Union, 1998–2026, https://eur-lex.europa.eu",
  "chunk_first_token_offset": 0,
  "chunk_last_token_offset": 743,
  "chunk_tokens": 743,
  "overlap_prev_tokens": 128,
  "language": "en",
  "authentic_language_note": "GDPR is multilingually authentic; the English text is one of 24 equally authentic versions."
}
```

**7. Rendering behavior in the revamp UI.** Citation format: `GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)`. Tap `[see law text ▸]` resolves via `legal_kb.retrieve(jurisdiction="EU", statute_id="GDPR", article="6", paragraph="1", point="a")`. Because **article numbers and paragraph/point numbering are stable across consolidated versions**, citations remain valid even if a future consolidation lands. **Recital numbers can shift** when new recitals are inserted mid-list on amendment — the citation format must NOT rely on recital numbers as primary anchors; if recitals are cited, render as "Recital 32 (GDPR, 2016-05-04 consolidation)" with the version-date qualifier so a future shift is auditable.

**8. Placeholder-to-real cutover checklist.**
- `data/legal_corpus/eu/gdpr/` — replace placeholder with `gdpr-consolidated-20160504.txt` (base) + `gdpr-recitals.txt` (separate). Include `ATTRIBUTION.md` with CC-BY-4.0 notice + fetch date.
- `data/legal_corpus/eu/gdpr/manifest.json` — records `celex_consolidated`, `fetch_date`, `sha256` of each file, list of amendments layered.
- `services/legal_kb.py::retrieve` — extend metadata payload to include `celex_consolidated`, `version_date`, `chapter_title`, and the `authentic_language_note`. Ensure hits return `article_paragraph` + `article_point` so the UI can build the anchor link.
- `AnalysisPayload.finding.legal_context_passages` — each passage carries `source_url` (with `#` anchor to the article), `citation_short` (e.g. "GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)"), `citation_long` (with version-date qualifier), and `attribution` string. Confirm the field already accepts `citation_short` — if not, extend the pydantic schema before cutover.
- Update `LIB-LEGAL` to record GDPR as "ingested — 20160504 consolidation" (currently "placeholder").
- Add jurisdiction code `EU-GDPR` to `.claude/skills/legal-kb` reference table (do NOT modify skill during this planning phase).

**9. Version-refresh policy.**
- **Yearly refresh floor** — refetch base consolidated text on Jan 15 each year and diff against stored SHA256.
- **Event-driven refresh** — subscribe to EUR-Lex RSS/OJ notifications for CELEX 32016R0679. Any corrigendum, amendment, or new consolidated version triggers refetch within 30 days.
- **On refresh, always reconcile:** article-number stability check (fail loud if Article count ≠ 99), recital-count check (currently 173 — flag change), and chunk-boundary diff report.

**10. Legal-review gates for owner (counsel questions).**
- (a) Is the attribution string "© European Union, 1998–2026, https://eur-lex.europa.eu, CC-BY-4.0" sufficient to satisfy CC-BY-4.0 attribution in an in-app tooltip rendering — or does counsel prefer a dedicated `/legal/attribution` page?
- (b) Do we surface only the English text, or must the UI expose a "view authentic version" toggle for all 24 EU languages? (Legally, all 24 are equally authentic.)
- (c) Is the "documentation-tool disclaimer" required to appear on every citation tooltip, or is it sufficient to display once per session in a footer/legal notice?
- (d) When we render an excerpt (chunk) rather than the full article, do we need "This is an excerpt; see EUR-Lex for the full article" boilerplate per chunk?

### EU AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

**1. Authoritative source URL (consolidated).**
- ELI landing: <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj>
- Consolidated text: <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20240712>
- Base act (as-published in OJ): <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=OJ:L_202401689>
- All CELEX identifiers verified via fetch 2026-07-04.

**2. License / re-use terms.** Same as GDPR — CC-BY-4.0 for editorial/consolidated text, Commission Decision 2011/833/EU as foundational policy, metadata CC0. Attribution string identical pattern: "© European Union, 1998–<current year>, https://eur-lex.europa.eu, CC-BY-4.0."

**3. Version identifier.** `02024R1689-20240712` — consolidated version dated 2024-07-12. **No 2026 consolidated version exists yet as of 2026-07-04.**

**Post-consolidation activity that must be layered manually until a fresh consolidation lands:**
- **Digital Omnibus (AI simplification package)** — Commission proposal adopted 2025-11-19; Council + Parliament political agreement 2026-05-07. Adjusts the timeline for high-risk-rule application by linking it to availability of harmonised standards, common specifications, or Commission guidelines. **When a delegated regulation formally amending Annex III or Article 113 lands in the OJ, layer it and record the CELEX.**
- **Commission guidelines on prohibited AI practices** (published Feb 2025) — interpretive, not amending. Store in a separate `guidelines/` subcorpus (see chunking).
- **Commission guidelines on the AI system definition** (published Feb 2025) — same treatment.
- **Draft GPAI guidelines** (published 2025-07-18) — draft only; do NOT ingest as authoritative. Track for status change.
- **Code of Practice on transparency** — track for adoption.

**Applicability dates that affect `effective_date` metadata:**
- Entry into force: 2024-08-01
- Prohibited-practices articles applicable: 2025-02-02
- Governance rules applicable: 2025-08-02
- Full applicability: 2026-08-02 (subject to Digital Omnibus deferral)

**4. Structural hierarchy.**
- 13 chapters (Chapter I → Chapter XIII)
- 113 articles (Art. 1 → Art. 113)
- 180 recitals (Recital 1 → Recital 180)
- 13 annexes (Annex I → Annex XIII), several of which are lists (e.g. Annex III enumerates high-risk AI system categories; Annex XIII enumerates GPAI systemic-risk criteria)
- Sub-article structure: paragraphs and points as with GDPR (e.g. Art. 6(2), Art. 51(1)(a))

**5. Chunking recommendation.**
- **Primary chunk unit: one Article.** Same 512–1024-token target, 128-token overlap.
- **Long articles: split at paragraph boundary.** Candidates include Art. 5 (prohibited practices — very long), Art. 6 (classification rules for high-risk), Art. 13 (transparency), Art. 50 (transparency obligations for certain AI systems), Art. 51 (GPAI classification), Art. 55 (GPAI-with-systemic-risk obligations), Art. 72 (post-market monitoring), Art. 99 (penalties).
- **Recitals: separate corpus** (180 recitals → 180 chunks or grouped by topic; recommend per-recital to preserve citation).
- **Annexes: separate corpus, one chunk per Annex item.** Annex III (high-risk categories) and Annex XIII (systemic-risk criteria) are enumerations where each numbered item is atomic — chunk per item, not per Annex.
- **Guidelines subcorpus:** Commission guidelines (prohibited-practices, AI-system-definition) chunked per section, tagged `chunk_type: "guideline"` and `authoritative_weight: interpretive` so retrieval can down-weight vs. articles.

**6. Metadata schema per chunk.** Same base schema as GDPR, plus:
```json
{
  "annex_number": "III",
  "annex_item": "6(a)",
  "chunk_type": "article" | "recital" | "annex" | "guideline",
  "authoritative_weight": "binding" | "interpretive",
  "applicability_date": "2026-08-02",
  "delegated_acts_layered": [],
  "implementing_acts_layered": [],
  "digital_omnibus_status": "pending — political agreement 2026-05-07, awaiting OJ publication"
}
```

**7. Rendering behavior in the revamp UI.** Citation format: `AI Act Art. 5(1)(a)` for articles; `AI Act Annex III (item 6)` for annex items; `AI Act Recital 27 (2024-07-12 consolidation)` for recitals. Tap `[see law text ▸]` resolves via `legal_kb.retrieve(jurisdiction="EU", statute_id="AI_ACT", article=..., paragraph=..., point=..., annex=..., annex_item=...)`. Because the **Digital Omnibus is expected to renumber or shift applicability dates but not article numbers**, article-anchored citations remain stable. **Recital-anchored citations must always carry the version-date qualifier.** Annex items are stable in numbering but their content can be amended via delegated act — surface `delegated_acts_layered` in the tooltip so the reader knows the source is current.

**8. Placeholder-to-real cutover checklist.**
- `data/legal_corpus/eu/ai-act/` — `ai-act-consolidated-20240712.txt` (articles) + `ai-act-recitals.txt` + `ai-act-annexes/annex-01.txt` … `annex-13.txt`. `ATTRIBUTION.md` + `manifest.json` (SHA256 per file).
- `data/legal_corpus/eu/ai-act/guidelines/` — subcorpus for Commission guidelines (prohibited-practices.txt, ai-system-definition.txt). Do NOT include the draft GPAI guidelines until finalized.
- `services/legal_kb.py::retrieve` — extend to support `annex` + `annex_item` args, and to filter by `authoritative_weight` (binding vs. interpretive).
- `AnalysisPayload.finding.legal_context_passages` — same schema extension as GDPR; add `authoritative_weight` field so the UI can render guideline hits with an "interpretive guidance" badge.
- Add jurisdiction code `EU-AI-ACT` to `.claude/skills/legal-kb` reference table.
- Update `LIB-LEGAL` to record AI Act as "ingested — 20240712 consolidation + [guidelines list]" (currently absent from LIB-LEGAL).

**9. Version-refresh policy.**
- **Quarterly refresh floor** — refetch consolidated text on Jan 15, Apr 15, Jul 15, Oct 15. The AI Act is under active regulatory development (Digital Omnibus, Codes of Practice, harmonised standards) — a yearly cadence is too slow.
- **Event-driven refresh** — subscribe to Commission "AI Office" newsroom + EUR-Lex RSS for CELEX 32024R1689 amendments. Digital Omnibus OJ publication is a high-priority trigger.
- **On refresh, reconcile:** article count (currently 113), annex count (currently 13), recital count (currently 180), applicability-date change (Art. 113), and Annex III item list.

**10. Legal-review gates for owner (counsel questions).**
- (a) Same CC-BY-4.0 attribution question as GDPR.
- (b) The Digital Omnibus reshapes applicability dates via linkage to standards availability. Should the tool render "applicable from 2026-08-02 (may be deferred per Digital Omnibus)" or hold off surfacing dates until the amendment is in force?
- (c) Commission guidelines are interpretive, not binding. Is a UI "interpretive guidance" badge sufficient legal disclaimer, or does counsel require an explicit "not legally binding" tooltip?
- (d) The draft GPAI guidelines — confirm counsel agrees we do NOT ingest drafts.
- (e) Annex III (high-risk categories) is the most-cited annex in a Terms-of-Service context. Confirm counsel-approved rendering when a finding maps to an Annex III item (e.g. how to phrase "this ToS involves an AI system in a high-risk category per AI Act Annex III(6)(a)" without giving legal advice).

### Scope decision — Data Act, DSA, DMA

The dispatch brief asks us to "check if in scope" for Data Act, DSA, and DMA. Recommendation below with rationale; final call is the owner's.

| Statute | CELEX | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Data Act — Regulation (EU) 2023/2854** | 32023R2854 | **IN scope (Tier 2 — after GDPR + AI Act).** | Directly governs contract terms between data holders and users. Prohibits unfair contractual terms unilaterally imposed on enterprises. A ToS analyzer inspecting B2B or connected-product contracts will need Data Act citations. Base URL: <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2854/oj>. Effective 2025-09-12; verify current consolidation state at ingestion time. |
| **DSA — Regulation (EU) 2022/2065** | 32022R2065 | **IN scope (Tier 1 — with GDPR + AI Act).** | Article 14 explicitly regulates the terms and conditions of intermediary services: must be clear, plain, intelligible, publicly available, machine-readable; users must be notified of significant changes. This is THE core EU statute on ToS transparency and is directly load-bearing for a ToS-analysis tool. Base URL: <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj>. 93 articles, 5 chapters, 156 recitals, no annex. |
| **DMA — Regulation (EU) 2022/1925** | 32022R1925 | **OUT of Tier-1 scope; MAYBE Tier 3.** | Applies only to designated gatekeepers (Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, ByteDance, Meta, Microsoft, Booking, and any future designations). Relevant only if the ToS being analyzed belongs to a gatekeeper — a narrow slice of the corpus. Recommend deferring until user demand emerges. Base URL: <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/1925/oj/eng>. If included, ingest per same pattern; Art. 5, 6, 7 are the operative obligation articles most relevant to ToS analysis. |

**Recommendation for the results-view revamp unblock (Q11=B):**
Ingest **GDPR + AI Act + DSA** in Tier 1. Data Act follows as Tier 2 (post-launch). DMA deferred to Tier 3 (demand-driven).

### Chunking strategy summary
- **Article-per-chunk primary** with paragraph-split for long articles. Never split mid-paragraph.
- **Recitals in a separate corpus** with per-recital chunks. Retrievable but tagged `authoritative_weight: interpretive` so results-view ranking prefers article hits.
- **Annexes chunked per numbered item** (not per annex), because Annex III of the AI Act and similar enumerations are semantically atomic at the item level.
- **512–1024 tokens target, 128-token overlap.** Overlap carries article header + first sentence forward for retrieval-time dedup.
- **Guidelines subcorpus** for AI Act interpretive guidance; tagged `binding: false`.
