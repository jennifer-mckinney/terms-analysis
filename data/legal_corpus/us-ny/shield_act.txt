## Section 899-aa — Notification of a security breach
[PLACEHOLDER] Any business that owns or licenses computerized data including private information of a New York resident must notify the resident of any breach of the security of the system following discovery.

## Section 899-bb — Data security requirements
[PLACEHOLDER] A business must develop, implement, and maintain reasonable safeguards to protect the security, confidentiality, and integrity of private information, including administrative, technical, and physical safeguards.
